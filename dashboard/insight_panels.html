Pattern discovery

DBSCAN groups failed deletions by which kinds of records failed and how. Groups that recur are patterns. Cases that fit no group, or that had no look-alike when they were audited, are the unseen ones.

Pattern catalogue

Sorted by size. A signature item is a policy category and how it failed. The core is every item carried by at least half the cases.

When each pattern appeared

One tick per failed case at its audit date. A diamond marks the date a pattern had four cases and became established. A ringed tick had no earlier look-alike.

The prompt behind the explanations

The model receives the audited case as structured facts and writes prose around them. It never sees the request text, an email, a name or a phone number, and it cannot change a status or a step.
